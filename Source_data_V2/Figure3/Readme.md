Figure 3. POPS reconstruction of a ward-based SARS-CoV-2 cluster in respiratory ICU BALF metagenomes.
Panel A shows stepwise filtering of contigs from co-assembly of 17 BALF metagenomes collected over 3 days. The initial co-assembly yielded 1,114 contigs. Each dot represents a contig; dot size indicates contig length and color indicates taxonomic assignment. Panels A1 through A3 show all contigs from the initial co-assembly, contigs retained after recurrence filtering, and contigs retained after additional control subtraction. Panels A4 through A6 compare the final retained contigs in cases and controls by mean coverage, fragment frequency, and mean sequencing depth (log10 scale), respectively.
Panel B shows the final retained SARS-CoV-2 fragments after POPS filtering. Two fragments spanning positions 3–22,916 and 22,901–29,872 covered 99.99% of the viral genome with 100% signal purity. Coverage tracks for the five COVID-19 cases are shown across the recovered genome, with gene annotations displayed below. Lineage assignment was consistent with Omicron BA.5.2.48.
