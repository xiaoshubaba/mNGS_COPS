Figure 2. POPS optimization.
Panel A shows the relationship between detected pathogen reads (log10 scale) and two performance metrics in single metagenomes (n=4,334): genomic coverage and signal purity. Curves indicate generalized additive model fits with 95% confidence intervals.
Panel B shows optimization of pooling size. Normalized genomic coverage, signal purity, assembly success, and the composite score are plotted across pooling sizes; the gray dashed line indicates the selected pooling size (P=5).
Panel C shows optimization of filtering parameters. Normalized genomic coverage, signal purity, assembly success, and the composite score are plotted across cutoff combinations, where X denotes the minimum number of cases containing a fragment and Y the maximum number of controls containing that fragment. The gray dashed line indicates the selected cutoff (X=2, Y=1).
