Supplementary Figure S2. Pairwise Jaccard similarity among resampled simulated outbreak clusters, stratified by study project. Simulated outbreak cluster size was fixed at 5 cases. Each point represents 1 pairwise comparison between accepted clusters within a project; white circles indicate the mean Jaccard similarity for that project; violin plots summarize the distribution; and crossbars indicate the median. Projects are ordered by mean Jaccard similarity. For clusters of 5 cases, a Jaccard value of 0 indicates no shared samples, whereas values of approximately 0.11, 0.25, 0.43, and 0.67 correspond to 1, 2, 3, and 4 shared samples, respectively. The resampling procedure constrained overlap (Jaccard <0.8), and most accepted clusters showed substantially lower pairwise similarity.
